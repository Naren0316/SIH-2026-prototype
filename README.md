## 🌄 About

**TransitMitra** is an **offline-first travel and safety platform** designed for tourists travelling through remote and low-connectivity regions.

It keeps essential travel information and emergency communication available even when the internet is unavailable.

---

## 💡 Key Features

### 🏨 Mitra Kiosk

Hotels and homestays can act as offline information hubs and transfer regional data to tourists.

### 🚌 Verified Transit & Fares

Access offline route and fare information to help tourists make safer and more informed travel decisions.

### 🆘 Store-and-Forward SOS

SOS messages can move through nearby devices and **Mitra Carrier** vehicles until connectivity is restored.

### 🗣️ Offline Language Assistance

Access useful travel phrases without depending on an internet connection.
